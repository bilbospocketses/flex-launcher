# Global Desktop Browser & Operating System Market Share Report

This document compiles worldwide market share data for desktop web browsers and underlying operating systems based on aggregated global web traffic metrics (StatCounter).

---

## 1. Global Desktop Browser Market Share

These statistics aggregate non-mobile web traffic across all desktop and laptop operating systems (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, and BSD variants).

| Browser | Worldwide Desktop Share | Core Rendering Engine | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Google Chrome** | **73.28%** | Blink (Chromium) | Market leader across almost all regions |
| **Microsoft Edge** | **10.46%** | Blink (Chromium) | Pre-installed default on modern Windows PCs |
| **Mozilla Firefox** | **5.31%** | Gecko | Independent engine; strong open-source following |
| **Apple Safari** | **5.24%** | WebKit | Default browser on macOS |
| **Opera** | **1.99%** | Blink (Chromium) | Includes Opera GX / Opera One |
| **Other / Niche** | **3.72%** | Various | Brave, Vivaldi, Tor, Floorp, Pale Moon, etc. |

### Key Market Takeaways

* **Chromium Dominance:** Chromium-based engines (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi) collectively account for nearly **90%** of all desktop web sessions worldwide.
* **Desktop Edge vs. Safari:** While Safari holds a major overall footprint on mobile (via iOS), **Microsoft Edge holds the #2 spot on desktop machines**, driven primarily by deep enterprise and consumer Windows penetration.
* **Alternative Engines:** Independent non-Chromium browser engines on desktop represent a modest slice: Gecko (Firefox, ~5.3%) and WebKit (Safari, ~5.2%).

---

## 3. Browser Distribution by Operating System

Browser rankings shift considerably when isolating specific desktop operating systems:

### macOS
* **Apple Safari:** Jumps to **30–38%** share due to native OS integration, power efficiency, and iCloud Keychain synchronization.
* **Google Chrome:** Remains dominant or a close competitor at **50–55%**.
* **Firefox & Others:** Comprise the remaining **7–12%**.

### Linux (Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch, Debian, etc.)
* **Mozilla Firefox:** Captures a far higher share (**~25–35%+**) because it serves as the pre-installed default browser across major distributions.
* **Google Chrome / Chromium:** Remains widely used (**~45–55%**).
* **Brave & Vivaldi:** See disproportionately higher adoption within privacy-focused Linux communities compared to their general market baseline.

### Windows
* **Google Chrome:** Leads decisively with **~68–74%**.
* **Microsoft Edge:** Represents **~14–17%** due to its role as the integrated Windows default.
* **Firefox:** Captures approximately **~4–6%**.

---

## 4. Regional Variations

* **North America:** Safari's desktop share generally hovers higher at **12–15%** due to higher per-capita Mac ownership, while Chrome's desktop share hovers in the **60–65%** range.
* **Europe:** Firefox typically retains a slightly higher share (**6–8%**) than its global average, particularly in Germany and Central Europe.
* **Asia & Latin America:** Chrome commands an overwhelming majority, often exceeding **75–82%** of all desktop web sessions.
